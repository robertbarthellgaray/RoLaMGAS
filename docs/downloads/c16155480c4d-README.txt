ReachStar’s Eight-Planet Grand Tour
Original RSS/Principia trajectory design by ReachStar on Bilibili; date-adjusted JPL patched-conic recreation in RoLaMGAS. This is not an exact Principia replay. Inner encounters differ by up to about one day; outer encounters shift by approximately 5–19 days. C3 is 13.43529 km²/s²; ideal injection from 200 km LEO is 3.81853 km/s. The two modeled solar corrections total 0.02727 m/s, not an n-body navigation budget; the source table lists 4.54 m/s of TCMs. Research altitude exceptions: Earth 130 km and Venus 250 km minimum, below standard gallery floors. Atmosphere, moons, heating and navigation uncertainty are excluded. Solar coasts meet at ideal planet centers; flyby altitudes are inferred asymptotic turns, not resolved finite-radius flybys. Desktop finite-radius export can add corrections.

Analysis CSV: JD(TDB), x/y/z km, vx/vy/vz km/s, heliocentric Ecliptic J2000. Ideal center-node solar coasts, not a collision-free Celestia export.
