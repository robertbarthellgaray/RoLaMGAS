## How RoLaMGAS may use this source

Keep these as historical, destination-specific mission estimates. The article
does not assign C3, departure declination, launch windows, or spacecraft
propulsion assumptions to these ranges, nor define the mass accounting in
enough detail to reproduce the orbital/flyby difference. It describes LVM3 as
still under development. These are not current provider guarantees.

Do not turn these ranges into payload-versus-C3 samples, infer launch capacity
from the orbital masses, or interpolate between destination columns. They are
not loaded into the numerical C3 capacity chart. A performance curve or a
fully specified injection state and launch mass is needed for that chart.

The text also reports PSLV-C11 delivering the 1,380 kg Chandrayaan-1 spacecraft
to a 250 × 23,000 km Earth-bound orbit. This is an injection example, not a
positive-C3 escape performance point.
